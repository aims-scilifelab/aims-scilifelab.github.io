A model that works in a paper does not always work in the clinic or the lab. We study
how models behave under distribution shift and transfer, how to quantify and calibrate
their uncertainty, and how to evaluate them in ways that predict real-world reliability.
Trustworthiness is a first-class research question for us, not an afterthought.
