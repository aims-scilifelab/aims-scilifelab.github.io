Much of biology and medicine is measured in more than one way at once — an image,
a molecular profile, a clinical record. We develop methods that learn **shared
representations** across these modalities, so that information from one can inform
another and so that models capture structure that no single view reveals on its own.
This includes self-supervised pretraining, cross-modal prediction and alignment,
and learned representations that transfer across tasks. Multimodality is a tool we
use widely — not the whole identity of the lab.
