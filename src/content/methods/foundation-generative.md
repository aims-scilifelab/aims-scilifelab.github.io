We study models that learn broadly reusable structure from large, heterogeneous data,
and generative models that can synthesise, complete or reason about it. Our interest
spans foundation and vision–language models, large-scale pretraining, and generative
modelling of biological and medical data — both as objects of methodological study
and as practical tools for scientific and clinical problems.
