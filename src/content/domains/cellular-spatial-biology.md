Cells are where a great deal of biology becomes measurable. We work with high-content
imaging such as Cell Painting and with spatial omics including spatial transcriptomics,
learning to read cellular phenotypes, states, morphology and spatial organization from
these rich readouts. This is a central area for the lab, anchored in Gisele Miranda's
research on cellular and spatial biology.
